# Your little jar

Tap where you want a jelly to drop, or hold and drag to aim. While holding, slide up into the × circle and release to cancel. The dotted guide shows the starting path; jelly physics decides where the piece settles.

Three touching jellies of one colour make a crowd. Tap any member to pop it. A bigger crowd brings a bigger Bloom, stronger clearing and more points. Matching newcomers refresh a crowd; neglect it for too many ordinary drops and it frosts in place. Nearby pops can shatter frost.

Fever rewards you with four special jellies. Each keeps its own colour, counts twice toward its crowd and keeps its bonus until popped. Build with them at your own pace. The bar spends one segment with each Fever drop.

Keep the pile below the dotted line. Crossing it gives one rescue drop to make room. The game has a visible warning and sound before the run ends. After a run, share or save a picture of your jar, score and biggest link.

Picnic and Minimal themes, sound volumes, a drop guide, reduced motion and optional gentle haptics are in options. Reset local data has a separate confirmation. Install updates over the existing Android app to retain your progress.

Friends are optional. Share your friend code, accept a request, then create a room and give its code to your friend. Tap a code to copy it. Your friend presses Ready; the host presses Start. Versus is a three-minute score race for two to five players with separate jars and live opponent scores; Co-op shares a jar and alternates drops. Solo always works offline.
